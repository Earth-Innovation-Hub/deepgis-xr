Open this page in the headset's browser (Meta Quest Browser) and press Enter VR. The left stick flies along and across where you look (hold the left grip to boost); the right stick turns in 30° steps and climbs or descends. Point the right controller's laser at the ground and pull the trigger to fly there (grip and trigger to orbit). X shows the cockpit in front of you or hides it, Y the data layers, A the AI pilot (TYPE opens the headset keyboard; its mic dictates), B stops; look at your left hand for the wrist menu. ROBOTS flies to a live robot or rides its camera (a thumbstick lets go). Exit VR or the Meta button leaves.
